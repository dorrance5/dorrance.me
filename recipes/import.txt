HOW TO MAKE A RECIPE IMPORT FILE FOR DORRANCE.ME/RECIPES

These instructions are for an AI assistant. A person wants to put recipes into the
Dorrance family recipe book at https://dorrance.me/recipes. They send you recipes in
any form: photos of cookbook pages, screenshots, links, typed or spoken notes. You turn
them into one import file. They open the recipe book, sign in, and import the file.

WHAT TO MAKE

One JSON file with every recipe in it. One file can hold one recipe or a whole cookbook.
Name it recipes-import.json if you can make files. If you can't, write the JSON in one
code block so the person can copy it and paste it into the import screen.

{
  "dorranceRecipes": 1,
  "recipes": [
    {
      "name": "Megan's zuppa toscana",
      "servings": "6",
      "tags": ["Soup"],
      "ingredients": [
        "5 strips bacon (thick-cut)",
        "1 lb Italian sausage (casings removed)",
        "1 small bunch kale",
        "Salt and pepper"
      ],
      "steps": [
        "Cut the bacon into small pieces. Cook it in a pot on medium-high for 5 minutes.",
        "Add the sausage. Cook about 15 minutes until browned and crispy."
      ],
      "notes": "Half-and-half works in place of cream.",
      "source": "https://www.saltandlavender.com/sausage-bacon-potato-and-kale-soup/"
    }
  ]
}

THE FIELDS

name         Required. The recipe's title, in sentence case ("Banana bread").
ingredients  Required, but it can be an empty list. One ingredient per item. Put the
             amount first: "2 cups flour", "1/2 tsp salt", "3 eggs". An ingredient with
             no amount is fine: "Salt and pepper".
steps        One step per item, in order. Leave out the numbers ("1.", "Step 2"). Write
             times as words a timer can find: "Bake for 25 minutes", "Simmer 10 to 15
             minutes". Leave the field out if the recipe has no steps.
servings     Text: "6", "4 to 6", "2 loaves". Leave it out if unknown.
tags         Categories, usually one: "Soup", "Dessert", "Crock pot", "Breakfast",
             "Grill", "Salad", "Bread". Keep them short. Leave the field out if none fits.
notes        Tips, swaps, the cook's comments. Leave it out if there are none.
source       Where the recipe is from: a link, or text such as "Betty Crocker Cookbook,
             page 112" or "Grandma Dorrance".

Leave out any field that would be empty. Do not add id, owner or shared: the recipe book
fills those in.

RULES

- Copy the recipe; do not invent. If a photo is cut off or hard to read, keep what you can
  read and say what was missing in your reply, not in the file.
- Fix obvious misreads from photos ("1 cup fl0ur" is "1 cup flour"). Keep the cook's words
  otherwise.
- A recipe that runs over two pages or two photos is one recipe.
- A page with two recipes is two recipes.
- An ingredient list with parts ("For the sauce", "For the topping") stays one flat list.
  Do not add heading lines. Put the part in parentheses at the end when it matters:
  "2 tbsp butter (for the sauce)".
- Use plain text: no Markdown, HTML or emoji inside the fields.
- For a whole cookbook, put every recipe in the same file. Up to 500 recipes fit in one
  import; make more files if there are more.
- From a link, use the recipe on the page, not the story above it. Set "source" to the
  link.

HOW THE PERSON IMPORTS IT

1. Open https://dorrance.me/recipes and sign in.
2. Open Settings and tap "Import recipes". (Or tap + and then "Want an AI to fill this out for you?")
3. Choose the file, or paste what you wrote.
4. Check the list, untick anything they don't want, and tap Import.

The recipes go into that person's book, shared with the family unless they switch that
off. A recipe that is already in their book by the same name starts unticked.
